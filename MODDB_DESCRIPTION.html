THE LONG ROAD AHEAD v2.3.1

CONTINUOUS ZONE-TO-ZONE TRAVEL & EXPEDITION SIMULATION

The Long Road Ahead is a comprehensive travel simulation and continuous Zone expedition overhaul for S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Anomaly 1.5.3 and S.T.A.L.K.E.R. G.A.M.M.A., inspired by the physical, unforgiving travel mechanics of Kingdom Come: Deliverance.


Ditch immersion-breaking instant teleports. In The Long Road Ahead, crossing the Zone takes real time, drains stamina and calories, advances the world clock, and tracks your physical movement across the Zone. Right-click any discovered map spot, fast-travel marker, or campfire, or hire a local guide to set out. Your journey traverses the Zone in accelerated time, plotting animated breadcrumb waypoints across your PDA map while dynamic threat detection actively scans nearby ALife simulation squads and mutant packs along your path.

NEW IN v2.3.1

NEW AUTHORITATIVE COMPANION MANUAL RECALL (\ KEY)

Completely overhauled companion recall initiated via the \ hotkey (default DIK_BACKSLASH = 43, fully synchronized with Fair Fast Travel MCM settings). Intercepts and replaces Fair Fast Travel's broken bring_companions_to_actor logic with TLRA's robust multi-member teleport system, ensuring stuck or separated companions are always summoned reliably.

FIX USEFUL IDIOTS STANCE & BEHAVIOR BYPASS

Useful Idiots splits companions into individual squads and locks them out of teleporting when set to wait, cover, relax, or patrol mode. Manual recall (forced = true) now deliberately bypasses Useful Idiots stance restrictions, allowing players to summon companions even if they are stuck in wait mode across levels or switch distance, while safely preserving quest hostage protections.

NEW ANTI-STACKING RADIAL PLACEMENT (1.8m – 3.0m)

Companions no longer spawn inside your body or stack into a single coordinate! TLRA computes staggered radial positions (1.8m to 3.0m) around the actor with validated AI navmesh vertex checks, completely eliminating companion physics collisions, mesh stacking, and terrain clipping.

FIX OFFLINE & CROSS-LEVEL ALIFE SYNCHRONIZATION

Safely teleports server entities via sim:teleport_object across level borders, invalidates stale offline caches (db.offline_objects, db.spawned_vertex_by_id), clears animation locks (safe_reset_animation), and smoothly restores upright standing posture.

NEW STANDALONE HOTKEY FALLBACK & HUD NOTIFICATIONS

Added a standalone on_key_release listener with UI dialog focus checking and 800ms debounce protection so manual recall operates reliably even if Fair Fast Travel is uninstalled or disabled. Added clean, non-intrusive on-screen HUD message feedback indicating the number of companions successfully recalled.

NEW IN v2.3.0

NEW DYNAMIC DURATION & REAL-TIME PROGRESSION

Travel duration is no longer fixed at a rigid 2 minutes regardless of distance. Travel time dynamically scales based on actual world vector distance, walking speed, active encumbrance, and time acceleration factors. Journeys across multiple territories feel substantial, perilous, and physically grounded.

NEW NATIVE DIET SYSTEM INTEGRATION

Full native integration with the Diet System mod: carbohydrates, fats, and protein reserves now deplete dynamically over the course of the journey based on exact game-time elapsed alongside vanilla/GAMMA thirst (actor_status_thirst) and sleep (actor_status_sleep).

NEW CARRY WEIGHT & OVER-ENCUMBRANCE PHYSICS

Real maximum carrying capacity is dynamically calculated from equipped suits, backpacks, belt artifacts, and active boosters. Traveling while heavily encumbered severely degrades movement speed and exponentially accelerates stamina and calorie consumption.

CORE REWORKED SURVIVAL ECONOMY

Removed illogical self-paid ruble charges for walking journeys—you now pay for the journey with physical calories, fatigue, hydration, and precious in-game daylight.

DYNAMIC EXPEDITION PATHING & PDA BREADCRUMBS


Your expeditions across the Zone are visualized live through the PDA map interface:


  • Animated Waypoint Breadcrumbs: Green breadcrumb dots plot your physical trajectory across the PDA map in real time, advancing smoothly as your virtual coordinates traverse the Zone.
  • Multi-Level Transit Routing: Long-distance journeys between non-adjacent levels (e.g., Cordon to Jupiter) chart an authentic route through real intermediate level transitions (Cordon → Garbage → Rostok → Army Warehouses → Red Forest → Jupiter), properly accounting for distance, elapsed time, and local threats.
  • Safe Journey Abort: Need to halt immediately? Press Escape or Backspace mid-journey to safely interrupt travel. Your character smoothly drops onto the nearest safe AI ground vertex with partial stamina refunds and defensive grace windows.
  • Custom Levels Support: Includes dynamic routing and door dictionaries for 5 custom community maps (Promzone, Grimwood, Poselok UG, Bunker A1, and Collider) via the optional FOMOD module.

REAL-TIME THREAT DETECTION & AMBUSHES


The Chernobyl Exclusion Zone never sleeps. While your character is on the road, TLRA's Real-Time Threat Detection (RTTD) engine actively probes the Zone around you:


  • Active ALife Simulation Scanning: Continuously evaluates a 100-meter virtual sphere around your moving coordinates, checking for hostile faction squads and roaming mutant packs.
  • Tactical Roadside Ambush Drop: When danger intercepts your path, the journey aborts with a cinematic fade-in, camera stabilization, emergency weapon restore, and an immediate defensive grace window so you are never killed before the screen fades in.
  • Dynamic Stealth Dodge Mechanics: Experienced stalkers can bypass hostile encounters without firing a shot. When danger is detected, the engine rolls an evasion check modified by:
    • Night Darkness: Up to +40% bonus in pitch-black conditions.
    • Heavy Weather: Rain, storms, and dense fog mask your movement and acoustic profile.
    • Camouflage Outfits: Ghillie suits and camouflage armors boost evasion odds.
    • Stalker Faction Heritage: Faction experience grants baseline evasion bonuses.
  • Atmospheric Roadside Events: Expeditions aren't just combat—experience sunny breaks piercing the clouds (+Morale), discovered forgotten stashes, radiation hot pockets, and scenic vista ridge pauses.

DYNAMIC SURVIVAL & DIET SYSTEM INTEGRATION


Survival in the Zone is physically grounded. Long treks extract a real toll on your character's body:


  • Native Diet System Support: Fully hooked into G.A.M.M.A.'s Diet System mod:
    • Carbohydrates: Rapidly depleted by physical walking and cardiovascular exertion.
    • Fats: Burned steadily to sustain long-distance overland treks.
    • Proteins: Degrade gradually during multi-hour cross-Zone expeditions.
  • Continuous Thirst & Fatigue: Thirst (actor_status_thirst) and Sleepiness (actor_status_sleep) accumulate smoothly based on exact game-time elapsed. Crossing several sectors will leave your stalker exhausted, dehydrated, and hungry.
  • Zero Illogical Ruble Fees: You no longer pay cash to yourself to walk. Fast travel costs are paid entirely in food, clean water, and daylight hours.

INVENTORY WEIGHT & OVER-ENCUMBRANCE DYNAMICS


Your pack weight directly governs how fast and safely you move:


  • Real Carrying Capacity Detection: Evaluates equipped exoskeletons, armored suits, hunting backpacks, belt artifacts, and active booster stims.
  • Dynamic Encumbrance Scaling:
    • Normal Load: Full walking pace (default 1.8 m/s) and baseline metabolic burn.
    • Heavy Load: Noticeable reduction in walking speed; elevated stamina and calorie consumption.
    • Overweight / Over-Encumbered: Movement slowed to a crawl; exponential calorie, water, and fatigue drain.

COMPANION SQUAD RECALL & USEFUL IDIOTS SYNCHRONIZATION


Full multi-member companion squad transitions and recall tools:


  • Manual Recall Hotkey (\ Key): Pressing \ summons companions directly to your position with server-side ALife migration (sim:teleport_object).
  • Useful Idiots Compatibility: Automatically bypasses stance blocks when manually recalled, clearing wait/cover/patrol flags so companions follow you immediately.
  • Anti-Stacking Radial Placement: Spawns squad members in a validated 1.8m to 3.0m radial arc around the player, preventing physics launches, mesh stacking, and collision deaths.
  • Animation & Stance Recovery: Resets stuck animpoints (smoking, sitting, guitar) and restores standing combat readiness.
  • Quest Hostage Protection: Hostages and VIP rescue targets are strictly excluded from teleportation.
