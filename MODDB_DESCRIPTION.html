WELCOME TO CONTINUOUS TRAVEL


The Long Road Ahead is a complete travel simulation overhaul inspired by the physical, unforgiving travel mechanics of Kingdom Come: Deliverance.


Ditch immersion-breaking instant teleports. In The Long Road Ahead, crossing the Zone takes real time, drains stamina and calories, advances the world clock, and tracks your physical movement across the Zone. Right-click any discovered map spot, fast-travel marker, or campfire, or hire a local guide to set out. Your journey traverses intermediate territories in accelerated time, plotting animated breadcrumbs across your PDA map while dynamic threat detection actively scans nearby ALife simulation squads and mutant packs along your path.



THE THREE PILLARS OF EXPEDITION SURVIVAL:



01. PHYSICAL TIME & GEOGRAPHY
Travel duration mathematically scales based on true 3D world vector distance, character walking speed, active encumbrance weight, and world time acceleration. Journeys across multiple regions feel substantial and perilous.



02. REAL-TIME THREAT RADAR (RTTD)
A virtual 100m detection sphere continuously scans moving ALife squads and mutant packs along your route. If hostiles intercept your travel line, you are forcefully pulled out of travel for dangerous roadside combat—unless you slip past under night or storm cover.



03. DYNAMIC SURVIVAL & DIET ECONOMY
No artificial money sinks for walking! You pay for expeditions with physical calories, hydration, and fatigue. Features native deep integration with the Diet System mod: carbohydrates, fats, and protein reserves burn in real time.



NEW IN v2.3.1


NEW AUTHORITATIVE COMPANION MANUAL RECALL (\ KEY)


Completely overhauled companion recall initiated via the \ hotkey (default DIK_BACKSLASH = 43, fully synchronized with Fair Fast Travel MCM settings). Intercepts and replaces Fair Fast Travel's broken bring_companions_to_actor logic with TLRA's robust multi-member teleport system, ensuring stuck, separated, or lost companions are always summoned reliably.



FIX USEFUL IDIOTS STANCE & BEHAVIOR BYPASS


Useful Idiots splits companions into individual squads and locks them out of teleporting when set to wait, cover, relax, or patrol mode. Manual recall (forced = true) now deliberately bypasses Useful Idiots stance restrictions, allowing players to summon companions even if they are stuck in wait mode across levels or switch distance, while safely preserving quest hostage protections.



NEW ANTI-STACKING RADIAL PLACEMENT (1.8m – 3.0m)


Companions no longer spawn inside your body or stack into a single coordinate! TLRA computes staggered radial positions (1.8m to 3.0m) around the actor with validated AI navmesh vertex checks, completely eliminating companion physics collisions, mesh stacking, and terrain clipping.



FIX OFFLINE & CROSS-LEVEL ALIFE SYNCHRONIZATION


Safely teleports server entities via sim:teleport_object across level borders, invalidates stale offline caches (db.offline_objects, db.spawned_vertex_by_id), clears animation locks (safe_reset_animation), and smoothly restores upright standing posture.



NEW STANDALONE HOTKEY FALLBACK & HUD NOTIFICATIONS


Added a standalone on_key_release listener with UI dialog focus checking and 800ms debounce protection so manual recall operates reliably even if Fair Fast Travel is uninstalled or disabled. Added clean on-screen HUD message feedback indicating the number of companions successfully recalled.



NEW IN v2.3.0


NEW DYNAMIC DURATION & REAL-TIME PROGRESSION


Travel duration dynamically scales based on actual world vector distance, walking speed, active encumbrance, and time acceleration factors. Journeys across multiple territories feel substantial, perilous, and physically grounded.

NEW CARRY WEIGHT & OVER-ENCUMBRANCE PHYSICS

NEW GROUNDED SURVIVAL ECONOMY (ZERO RUBLE WALKING CHARGES)


Reworked travel economy removes illogical self-paid ruble charges for walking journeys. You pay for expeditions with your own physical calories, hydration, and in-game time.

REAL-TIME THREAT DETECTION & ROADSIDE AMBUSHES


The Chernobyl Exclusion Zone never sleeps. While your character moves along the virtual path, TLRA continuously performs proximity threat sweeps:


◈ Dynamic Threat Radius: Continuously scans for hostile stalker squads, mutant packs, and bandits within a 100m radius of your travel vector.


◈ Roadside Interceptions: If a hostile pack crosses your route, travel instantly drops. You are placed on the terrain ready for immediate combat with your weapon drawn.


◈ Weather & Darkness Stealth Mechanics: Experienced stalkers can evade detection. Your baseline stealth dodge chance is dynamically boosted by environmental cover:



• Clear Daylight: 25% Baseline Dodge Chance
• Pitch-Black Night: +20% Stealth Bonus (45% Total)
• Heavy Fog / Driving Rain: +25% Stealth Bonus (50% Total)
• Thunderstorms & Hurricanes: +40% Stealth Bonus (65% Total)


◈ Surge & Storm Interruption: Blowouts, Emissions, and Psi-Storms immediately abort travel and deposit you at the nearest terrain node so you can desperately sprint for hardened cover.



DYNAMIC SURVIVAL & DIET SYSTEM INTEGRATION


Survival in the Zone is physically grounded. Long expeditions demand thorough planning and adequate provisions:


◈ Time-Scaled Caloric Burn: Calories burn realistically based on exact trip length and elapsed in-game hours.


◈ Diet System Deep Hooks: Native hooks into the Diet System mod dynamically deplete carbohydrates, fats, and protein pools based on metabolic exertion.


◈ Hydration & Fatigue: Water stores decrease steadily, and sleep debt mounts on marathon marches across multiple regions.


◈ Starvation / Dehydration Protection: Travel will never silently kill you—if metabolic stats hit critical thresholds, emergency travel interruption deposits you safely to consume rations.



INVENTORY WEIGHT & OVER-ENCUMBRANCE DYNAMICS


Your pack weight directly governs how fast and safely you can cross the Zone:


◈ True Capacity Calculation: Dynamically detects effective carrying capacity from equipped exoskeletons, suits, upgraded backpacks, artifact belt configurations, and active stimulants.


◈ Progressive Speed Degrade: Encumbrance above 75% gradually degrades virtual travel speed from 1.8 m/s down to 0.8 m/s.


◈ Exhaustion Multiplier: Traveling while overweight dramatically multiplies fatigue rate and caloric expenditure.


◈ Hard Limit Safeguard: Severe over-encumbrance beyond critical limits prevents fast travel entirely, demanding stash management or companion load distribution.
